Conjuror is designed to work locally on your Mac. It searches installed applications, performs calculations, finds emoji, and asks macOS to run the Apple Shortcuts you choose. Search text is not sent anywhere.

Optional currency conversion downloads exchange-rate snapshots from [Frankfurter](https://frankfurter.dev) at most once per day. The latest snapshot remains available offline, and turning currency conversion off removes its cached rates.

This page will be updated with the full privacy policy before a public release. Questions can be raised through the [Conjuror GitHub repository](https://github.com/conjuror-app/conjuror).
